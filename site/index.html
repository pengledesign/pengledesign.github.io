How it works

From call to covered, fast.

Four steps, and we handle the heavy lifting.

  1. Price it out

    Build your quote on this page in a minute, or call and we'll size it with you.

  2. We drop and set up

    We deliver the tower, level it, aim the cameras, and get it online. No power hookup needed.

  3. We watch. You can too.

    Alerts hit your phone, or our team if you add monitoring. Plus a login to see your cameras from anywhere.

  4. Done? We pick it up.

    When the job wraps, let us know. No contract to break and no buyout.
