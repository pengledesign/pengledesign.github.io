Goblin Gauntlet

Goblin Gauntlet: Path of Plunder is a tabletop adventure RPG for anyone looking for a way into more complex role-playing games like Dungeons & Dragons, without needing a whole weekend to learn the rules. Everything in the box was designed from scratch: the packaging, the loot, danger, item and gear cards, the character sheets for each goblin, and the modular board tiles the party fights its way across.
