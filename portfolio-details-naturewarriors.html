Nature Warriors

Don’t be afraid of these friendly foresters unless you plan to harm Mother Nature. They serve as the forest’s guards, and they get increasingly violent the worse your offense. Twelve characters, from stump-headed brawlers to a moss-cloaked crow, each built from the plants and critters they protect.
